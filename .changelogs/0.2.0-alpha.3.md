Third alpha of GTNH Planner: plan GT New Horizons factories in game, from NEI.

**This is an alpha.** Expect rough edges, and keep a backup of your world's planner saves (`gtnhplanner/plans.dat`, backed up automatically to `backups/` beside it). Bugs and ideas: the new feedback key on the planner's top bar, or GTNH Planner's thread on the GT New Horizons Discord (the Discord key).

**Made for GTNH 2.9.** This build is made against GTNH 2.9.0-RC-2's mods (NEI 2.8.155, ModularUI2 2.3.92, GTNHLib 0.11.52, GT5-Unofficial 5.09.54). It is not for GTNH 2.8; stay on 0.2.0-alpha.1 there.

## New

- **Feedback from the game**: the red bug key on the top bar sends a bug, an idea or a question from the planner, with a picture of your plan and the end of your log (names, folders and addresses taken out). No account needed; you get a link to follow it. If the game crashed while the planner was open, it offers to send that crash next time.
- **GregTech machines worked out exactly**: every GregTech card now has the machine's real settings (coil, hatches, amps and each machine's own options) and its real maths: parallels, overclocks, heat bonuses, exact recipe times and power. A new multiblock starts with sensible hatches.
- **The working, beside the settings**: a card's gear sheet shows how its numbers come about (supply, heat, discount, parallels, overclocks, time, draw and recipes a second) in plain words.
- **Tree Growth Simulator and Bacterial Vat** work their outputs out from your setup: the simulator's tools and each Forestry sapling's genes; the vat's glass, radiation and radio hatch, and it holds when they say it cannot run.

## Changed

- To share one machine between recipes, merge two cards; the card menu's Add another recipe is gone.
- The top bar's icons are redrawn as one crisp set.
- The tour's settings step shows a machine's working.

## Upgrading

Plans from earlier alphas open as before; a machine's old heat setting becomes the matching coil.

## Install

1. Drop `gtnhplanner-0.2.0-alpha.3.jar` into your GTNH 2.9 instance's `mods` folder, replacing any older `gtnhplanner` jar (client only; servers don't need it).
2. Open your inventory: the GTNH Planner button is at the bottom left.
